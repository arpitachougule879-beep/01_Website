Who I Am
The person behind the numbers

I am a dedicated Billing Engineer and Quantity Surveyor with over 3 years of experience in India's construction industry. My work lies at the intersection of architecture, finance, and precision — transforming blueprints into reliable cost intelligence that helps clients and contractors make confident decisions.

From government banking infrastructure to luxury residential bungalows and educational institutions, I have delivered BOQ and estimation services across project values ranging from ₹1.4 Crore to ₹80 Crore. Every project, regardless of scale, receives the same meticulous attention to detail.

Currently working with MitiMitra Consultant Pvt. Ltd., I collaborate with leading consultants and contractors across India to ensure every quantity take-off, rate analysis, and tender package meets the highest professional standards — minimizing cost overruns and bringing clarity to construction finances.

Accuracy in estimation is not just a skill — it's the foundation that every successful project is built upon.
